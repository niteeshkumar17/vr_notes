## 2. Biotechnology preview (later block) (ST-04-02)

After outer space, class will jump to the opposite extreme — **molecular** sizes.

**Biotechnology** is not a literal “biology + technology.” Functionally: **all the tools and techniques** through which humans change the **rate and direction of evolution** of a living organism — and can even cause **extinction**. Class: that is “playing God / nature.”

**Evolution** (class): gradual and **accumulative** changes towards **better adaptability** to the environment. Everything evolves — living, non-living, culture, language, even the Civil Services Examination.

**Cotton → bollworms → BT cotton**

- Cotton’s damaging pests = **bollworms** (larvae of a moth). They feed on cotton **bolls** and cut productivity. (Audio: “ball bombs” / “bonbons” = bollworms.)
- Given centuries, cotton *might* evolve resistance. We do not wait.
- **BT cotton** is the biotech shortcut: **gradual → quick**. Class: **BT here is not the word “biotechnology.”** (Sheet did not expand the letters; do not invent a full form until next class.)
- Knock-on: cotton becomes non-edible to the pest → bollworm numbers fall → birds that eat them are hit. One gene edit moves a **food web**.

**CA hooks for later:** India–United States trade deal and **genetically modified (GM)** crops; **GM mustard**; **messenger Ribonucleic Acid (mRNA)** cancer therapy; **ethanol** from molasses / maize / rice (productivity of feedstock).

## 4. What “space” means — Karman Line and the Outer Space Treaty (ST-04-03, ST-04-04)

**Space** (generic) = any **three-dimensional unoccupied** area. **Space technology** always means **outer space**.

Walk up from Earth’s curvature. Imaginary boundary at **100 km** = **Kármán / Karman Line**, proposed by **Theodor Karman** (class: Hungarian physicist — no biography needed).

| Domain | Where | Activities | Sovereignty |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **Aeronautical space** | **Below** the line | Needs **air** — aircraft | **Yes.** Airspace belongs to the state underneath. Class joke: BrahMos “evening walk” over Pakistan. |
| **Outer / astronautical / astronomical space** | **Beyond** the line | Spacecraft, satellites | **No** sovereign claim. Common property of humankind. **No military base** on Moon / Mars / an asteroid. |

**100 km is not rigid.** **National Aeronautics and Space Administration (NASA)** / United States treats it as **80 km**. Lies “largely in that zone.”

**United Nations Outer Space Treaty (OST), 1967** — Cold War, fear of space militarisation. Class: **non-binding / non-enforceable** — moral pressure. That is why militarisation is creeping back.

## 5. What a satellite is — and the three application families (ST-04-05)

**Generic satellite** = any entity whose **identity is derived around another body**. NCR cities are satellites of Delhi. Moon is Earth’s satellite; Earth is the Sun’s. **Microsatellite Deoxyribonucleic Acid (DNA)** in biology uses the same word with no orbit.

**Here:** **artificial / application satellites**, **majorly** around Earth — not absolutely. **Chandrayaan** orbits the Moon; **Mangalyaan** orbited Mars; **Shukrayaan** would orbit Venus.

Value is not “a new application terrestrial tech cannot do.” Value is **coverage** → **cheaper** service. Higher altitude → bigger footprint.

| Family | What it does | Class applications |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Communication** | Links | **70–75%** of use = **TV broadcast**. Also **tele-education**, **tele-medicine** (Siachen soldier guided from a city), **satellite-based internet services (SBIS)** — **Starlink** most numerous / successful. |
| **Remote sensing (RS)** / **Earth Observation Satellite (EOS)** | Sense from a distance | Forestry, agriculture (this year’s GS-I), geology / minerals, disaster management, weather, **military surveillance** + **surgical-strike precision**. |
| **Navigation** | **Position and timing** in real time | The blue dot + “you will be there by…”. Essay hook: tech making humans loners. Class: siblings in **Uttar Pradesh (UP)** guided onto a half-built bridge. |

**Trap:** an **EOS is necessarily** a remote-sensing satellite. A remote-sensing satellite is **not necessarily** Earth-sensing — **Chandrayaan** remote-senses the **Moon**.

## 6. Why 100 km — orbital velocity, fuel, collision avoidance (ST-04-06)

**Orbit** = the path. Each orbit needs a **unique orbital velocity**. More or less → you leave that orbit.

Sheet: **~30 km** holds **99.99%** of Earth’s atmosphere. Audio: **under the Karman Line** sits **nearly 100%** of the atmosphere (gravity glues it). **International Space Station (ISS)** class altitude: **~400 km**.

**Thought experiment:** plant a satellite at **30 km** vs **400 km**, both given the “right” speed.

- **30 km:** air **resists** → speed drops → only fix is **burn fuel continuously** → tank empty in **minutes**. You do not spend billions for minutes. **No satellite stays in orbit powered by fuel.**
- **Beyond atmosphere:** give the velocity **once**. **Inertia** keeps it going unless an external force acts.

That is **why** outer space is defined where it is: **beyond Karman, motion is not fuel-powered.**

**Satellites still carry fuel** — for three jobs only:

1. **Acquire initial velocity**
2. **Change orbit** — **orbit raising** (higher) / **orbit lowering** (lower). Today’s GISAT: **transfer orbit** = temporary; must be raised.
3. **Collision Avoidance Manoeuvre (CAM)** — dead objects at **25–30,000 km/h**, millions of them. Satellite must “dodge.” Fuel for CAMs.

Fuel quantity is a **strong determinant of lifespan**. First mistake after dry tanks is the last. World (including India) is working on **on-orbit refuelling** — not operational in class’s “at the moment.”

## 7. History — balloons, sounding rockets, Space Race 1.0 (ST-04-07)

Astronomy is as old as humankind. **Space technology** is ~a century old.

**1930s (audio; sheet wrote 1950):** United States sent **hot-air balloons** — first military surveillance (height = coverage), then **scientific instruments** for **vertical profiling** (layer-by-layer: thermometer, pH, carbon dioxide (CO₂), magnetic field, particles). **Two limits:** very **slow**; ceiling ~**25 km** (outer pressure falls, balloon bursts / drops). Not outer space — but it posed the problem.

**Sounding rockets** — faster + higher; still carry instruments for **atmospheric investigation**. **“Sounding”** is figurative: a **naval** term (sound waves for **underwater** vertical profiling). Not because the rocket uses sound. **India / ISRO:** **Rohini** series of sounding rockets (regular). **Do not confuse** with **Rohini satellites**.

**Space Race 1.0** = **military hostility + geopolitical antagonism + high optics** (whatever goes up is visible). Tool of **upmanship**, not humanity. Expect a Mains “**then vs now**” question.

| Who / when | What (class) |
|:---|:---|
| **Union of Soviet Socialist Republics (USSR), 1957** | **Sputnik** — first artificial satellite. US President: “just one small ball in the air.” Optics = nationalism. |
| USSR | First **man** in space — **Yuri Gagarin** (audio: “Gaginam”) |
| USSR | First **woman** — **Valentina Tereshkova** |
| USSR | **Laika** — street dog, not a trained space dog; **not brought back** (planned sacrifice). Books / films later. |
| **John F. Kennedy (JFK)** | Moon not because it is easy — because it is **hard**. Finish line shifted after USSR’s early wins. |
| **16 July 1969** | **Apollo 11** lifts off |
| **20 July 1969** | **Man on Moon** (“one small step…”) |
| **1972 · Apollo 17** | **Abrupt end.** USSR did not contest the lunar finish line. NASA later: **>60%** of budget on a mission not justified by human value. Then **five decades** with **no** human lunar landing. |

**Apollo** = Greek sun god. Isolated / **separate** missions — each crew had its own objectives.

**Sputnik** as a symbol: Russia named its Covid vaccine **Sputnik**.

## 8. Space Race 2.0 / Lunar Race — Artemis, ILRS, India (ST-04-08)

**2015:** China announces a **human-to-Moon** goal. Next year, Trump government: we go **first**. **Space Race 2.0** = **Lunar Race**.

**Artemis** = **twin sister of Apollo** (Greek). Female name = **gender-inclusive** symbolism.

| Mission | Class facts |
|:---|:---|
| **Artemis 1 (2022)** | **Unmanned.** **Orion** spacecraft: to the Moon, orbit, return. |
| **Artemis 2 (1 April 2026)** | **Manned.** **No landing.** Orbit + return. **Farthest** humans have travelled (wider lunar loop than Apollo). Crew named in class: **Christina Koch** — first **woman** to the Moon (fly-by); **Victor Glover** — first **Black** man (US briefings repeated it until it felt token; Glover’s Earth-from-Moon clip: we see **Homo sapiens**, not races). |
| **Artemis 3 / 4…** | **Humans on Moon by 2028** (US target — China said it would land **after** that year, so US is hurrying). |
| **Base camp ~2032** | **Sustained human presence.** NASA Administrator (class: **Jared Eisenman**): not **“flags and footprints”** — **stay**. Crew landings every **six months** after Artemis 5; “America will never again give up the Moon.” |

**Apollo vs Artemis (Mains hinge):** Apollo missions were **isolated**. Artemis is **additive** — each flight builds the next.

**Why a lunar base (class order):**

1. **Research** — lunar atmosphere, soil, geology, helium, water (open-ended).
2. **Space tourism** (not yet for the Moon).
3. **Springboard to Mars** — the **main** reason. Lower gravity; use lunar **water → fuel**; then hop.

**United States “Gateway”:** an orbital platform around the Moon was the original stepping stone (Earth → Gateway → surface). **Week before Artemis 2** they dropped the mediator — **direct landing**. Class: that is why Gateway was not taught as current US plan.

**China (2031) + Russia → International Lunar Research Station (ILRS)** — could be an **orbital platform** *or* a **surface base camp**. Still waiting. Goal: same — **sustained human presence**.

**India is not a participant in the race.** Write that **categorically** in answers. **Vikram Sarabhai** (father of the Indian space programme): invest in space only for **national development**, not power or prestige = **Sarabhai Doctrine**. India has still **walked ahead** of a pure-development reading — **humans to the Moon** is a goal (sheet: **2040**). Practical brake: **Gaganyaan** not done; senior-scientist resignation / delays. Not racing is, for now, also a **compulsion** (we do not run that fast).

## 9. “House in space” — space station (started; unfinished) (ST-04-09)

After the Moon, the next prestige target was **who builds a house in space first**. No land to buy — make it **orbit**.

**Space station / orbital platform** = platform for **sustained human presence** in space. Also a **research** platform. Tourism was not the 1970s reason.

**Prelims trap (class wrote a four-option question):** valuable because of (1) **zero gravity** (2) **weightlessness** (3) **high-frequency radiation**.

| Claim | Verdict |
|:---|:---|
| **Zero gravity** | **Wrong.** If gravity were zero the station would **escape** Earth. Gravity is what **binds** it. You get **microgravity** — very small, **not zero**. Gravity falls with distance. |
| **Weightlessness** | **Correct.** Station is in **continuous free fall**. Weight is felt as the **recoil** of a surface. No platform “pushing back” → no weight. ISS has been “falling” for **over two decades** without hitting Earth because **orbital velocity** makes the **curve of fall parallel to Earth’s curve**. |
| **High-frequency radiation** (ultraviolet (UV), X-rays, …) | **Correct.** Outside the atmosphere those frequencies are **accessible**. |

**Orbital-velocity recap (Newton’s tower):** throw harder → land farther; give the **specific** velocity → you keep “falling around” the planet. Velocity is a **vector**; gravity still pulls; the path is curved **because** of gravity.

**National stations (class paused here):**

- **United States — Skylab**
- **USSR — Mir** (audio: “Neel”)

## Prelims traps (class)

- **Karman = 100 km**; **NASA / US ≈ 80 km** — not a brick wall.
- **OST 1967 is non-binding.** No sovereign claim; no mili-base on celestial bodies — **moral**, not a police force.
- **EOS ⊂ remote sensing**; Chandrayaan is RS, **not** an Earth-observation sat.
- Satellites do **not** stay up by burning fuel. Fuel = start + raise/lower + **CAM**.
- **Three** sats at **35,786 km** (equilateral, same orbit) → global cover.
- Space station ≠ **zero gravity**. **Microgravity + weightlessness + radiation.**
- **Rohini sounding rockets ≠ Rohini satellites.**
- **BT** in BT cotton **≠** the word biotechnology.
- **India is not in the space race** (Sarabhai Doctrine) even if a **2040** human-Moon goal exists.
