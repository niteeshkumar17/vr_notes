## 1. The tree, and what “recombinant” means (ST-13-01)

He opened with the classification. A **recombinant vaccine** is a vaccine developed with the tools of biotechnology: **recombinant DNA technology**, or **genetic engineering**. Three families sit under that word.

| Family | What he named | What he did not open |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Recombinant vector** | **Viral vector** vaccine, and **bacterial vector** vaccine | How the vector carries the gene |
| **Nucleic acid** | **mRNA** vaccine, and **DNA** vaccine, also called a **recombinant plasmid DNA** vaccine | How the blueprint is delivered |
| **Subunit** | **Polysaccharide**, **peptide**, and **conjugate** | Which antigen each one uses |

The audio said “contributed” for the third subunit. The sheet writes **conjugate**. Lock conjugate.

Beside the recombinant family stand the two whole-virus vaccines he actually compared: the **live attenuated vaccine (LAV)** and the **killed or inactivated vaccine**. The outcome of any vaccine is still **vaccine-induced immunity**, both **cell-mediated** and **humoral**. Humoral immunity blocks the virus from attaching. If the virus has already entered, the T-cells kill the infected cell, so infection need not become disease. That mechanism is Lecture 2 and Lecture 3.

## 2. Live attenuated: strong, and the least safe (ST-13-02)

A live attenuated vaccine is a **weakened, replicating vaccine virus**. It is not dead. Once it is in the body it multiplies, and that multiplication is what triggers the response. The worked example is the **oral polio vaccine (OPV)**. Drops go in at the mouth, pass the throat, and the virus multiplies in the intestine. It is the nearest safe stand-in for the live virus. The live virus itself would cause the disease.

**In a healthy body** the replication is **controlled**. Because the virus keeps multiplying, the antigen stimulation is **prolonged**. **Memory cells** form, and the vaccine-induced immunity is **stronger**, both cell-mediated and humoral. Those memory cells clear the vaccine virus in the process.

**In an immunocompromised body** the same virus is not controlled. He meant a person whose immunity is already broken by a pre-existing condition: **cancer, HIV/AIDS, or an autoimmune disease**. Comorbid, weaker immunity. Replication is **uncontrolled**. The virus gets time to **mutate**. Virulence, the harmful nature that had been taken out when the live virus was turned into a vaccine virus, can **come back**. The vaccine then causes the infection it was meant to prevent. That is a **vaccine-derived infection (VDI)**.

For polio, the disability is paralysis of the muscles of the hands and legs. It is **not** caused by the wild virus. Two names:

- **Vaccine-associated paralytic poliomyelitis (VAPP)**
- **Vaccine-derived poliovirus (VDPV)**

He called this very rare: **one in 10 million** cases. It cannot be ruled out. A country that has eliminated wild polio still has to stop even that one case. While OPV is in use, VDPV remains possible. His order of work: protect the many first, then close the one-in-10-million gap. Polio virus is **not** a fast-mutating virus, unlike influenza and the coronavirus. A student had asked whether a vaccine virus that regains virulence would race through the population. He said it can spread, and it is not that kind of virus.

The note he dictated:

- A live attenuated vaccine should **never** be given to an immunodeficient or immunocompromised person, because the replicating vaccine virus can mutate, regain virulence, and cause a vaccine-derived infection.
- It is also **not given to pregnant women**, because it can infect the **fetus**.
- Compared with the other types, this is the **strongest protection** and the **least safe**.

## 3. Killed vaccine, and why the oral drop does two jobs (ST-13-03)

A killed or inactivated vaccine is a **non-replicating** vaccine virus. The virus has been killed by **chemical treatment**. It is dead, and it is static. In a healthy person and in an immunocompromised person the result is the same: **no replication, no mutation, no return of virulence**, and therefore **no vaccine-derived infection**. It can be given to both. It is **safer** than a live attenuated vaccine. The strength of the immunity is **lower**, because a dead virus does not keep stimulating the antigen. It still carries its biological identity, the antigen protein, so a response still happens.

OPV is given **by mouth**. The replicating vaccine virus builds **intestinal immunity**, also called **gut immunity**. A person who later meets the wild virus does not let that virus multiply in the intestine, and does not **excrete** it. The chain of transmission breaks. That is protection of the vaccinated person, and **indirect protection of people who were never vaccinated**.

He dictated the definition. **Population immunity**, also called **herd immunity** or **community immunity**: when a **significant proportion** of individuals in the population are vaccinated, it transfers **indirect protection** to the non-vaccinated **by breaking the chain of transmission** of the virus.

So OPV has a **dual role**: **individual immunity and population immunity**.

The other polio vaccine is injectable. **Inactivated poliovirus vaccine (IPV)** is a **dead** poliovirus. It does **not** build intestinal immunity. The vaccinated person develops antibodies and does **not** get paralysed. The wild virus can still **pass through** that person and circulate, and it can still reach someone who was never vaccinated. IPV does **not** build population immunity. It is **safer** than OPV. It can be given to a person with cancer. There is no vaccine-derived infection.

| | **OPV** | **IPV** |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Route | **Oral** | **Injectable** |
| Immunity | **Individual and population** | **Individual only** |
| Cost per dose | **Lower** | **Higher** |
| Who can give it | Basic training is enough | **Trained expertise** |

The path he wants remembered: start with **OPV**, so the population builds intestinal immunity and new cases stop. Then **OPV plus IPV**. Then **only IPV**, so even the one-in-10-million case is closed. Developed economies such as the **United States have used IPV since 2000**, because they had eliminated polio in **2000**. India is already using **both**. India still cannot drop OPV, because **Pakistan and Afghanistan** remain endemic and the virus can cross the border. When those two are certified polio-free, India can stop the oral vaccine. The 2011 West Bengal case, the three clear years, and the **2014** certification stay Lecture 3. He said “WTO” for the certifying body. That body is the **World Health Organization (WHO)**.

## 4. Why India was told it could not finish the job (ST-13-04)

There was a time, not very long ago, when **India reported 60 per cent of global polio cases**. He called that a global public health challenge. Later in the same hour he also said “more than 50 per cent”, while comparing India’s size with countries in Africa, including Nigeria. The figure he stated first, and repeated as the challenge, is **60 per cent**. He did not give the year. Do not supply one. The sheet has a year that the audio never landed.

The challenges, as he listed them:

- **High population density.**
- **Poor sanitation.** An infected person excretes the virus. Contaminated water, including sewage mixing with drinking water, carries it on. The route is **faeco-oral**. The virus enters by the mouth.
- **Malnutrition and diarrhoea.** An oral vaccine has to be absorbed. In a child with diarrhoea it may not be, so intestinal immunity does not form. He did not add “overcrowding” as a separate heading. Density is already the first point.
- **Inaccessible terrain.** Health workers go by boat, and then have to persuade the household.
- **People’s participation.** Doors are shut. This is the vaccine-hesitancy line on the sheet.
- **Migrant labour.** A child needs the **complete set of doses**, from **0 to 5 years**. An oral vaccine needs more doses than an injectable one. That dose fact is already Lecture 3.
- **Cold chain.** OPV is stored and carried at about **2 to 4 °C**, with ice packs, or it loses efficacy.

Because of the caseload and these obstacles, the global opinion was that India would **never** eliminate polio. The people who closed it were the **Accredited Social Health Activists (ASHA)**. He called them the foot soldiers: remote areas, a shut door, and a return visit until the child was vaccinated.

The **Global Polio Eradication Initiative (GPEI)** was launched in **1988**. That year the world reported **3.5 lakh** polio cases. In **2018** the global figure was **33 cases**, and those were in **Pakistan and Afghanistan**. That is a reduction of **more than 99 per cent**. It was a collaboration of the **WHO**, the **United Nations Children’s Fund (UNICEF)**, the **Bill and Melinda Gates Foundation**, and **Rotary International**, which he described as a United States-based non-governmental organisation. The same effort funded India’s **pulse polio** campaign, which India started in **1995**. Do not collapse 1988 and 1995 into one date. He said the 33 cases of 2018 were the global remainder, not an Indian outbreak.

The takeaway he wanted: a global public-health challenge became a global public-health success, and the same mass-campaign lesson is what India still needs for **tuberculosis**. India as the TB capital, and the Sustainable Development Goal of ending the epidemic by **2030**, stay Lecture 3. This hour only pointed at them.

The wild virus, so the reason for all of this, is an **RNA** virus. It passes **mouth, throat, intestine**. In the intestine it replicates and causes **vomiting and diarrhoea**. It may then enter and damage the **motor neurons of the spinal cord**, and paralyse the muscles of the **hands and legs**. That disability is **permanent**. There is **no cure**. The only tool is prevention, which means the vaccine, which means breaking the chain before the virus reaches those neurons.

## 5. A protein is a chain, and life does not run without one (ST-13-05)

He turned to protein because the recombinant and the central-dogma hours need it. A **protein** is a **polypeptide chain of amino acids**. The building block is the amino acid, as glucose is the unit of a carbohydrate. Two amino acids joined by a **peptide bond** are a **dipeptide**. Three are a **tripeptide**. Many are a **polypeptide**.

There are **20** amino acids. A chain can be far longer than 20, because an amino acid can repeat. He said a chain can be of the order of seven, and it can run to **1,000 or more**. The **spike protein** of SARS-CoV-2, already in Lecture 1, is a chain of about **1,000** amino acids.

Life cannot exist without protein. The four examples:

| Protein | What it does |
|:---|:---|
| **Haemoglobin** | A **transport** protein. An **oxygen-carrying, iron-containing** protein inside the **red blood cell**. Liver and heart muscle need that oxygen |
| **Antibodies** | An **immunoprotein**. They block virus infection. Defensive proteins already in the blood |
| **Enzymes** | **All enzymes are proteins.** They digest food. **Amylase** digests carbohydrate: **starch to glucose**, and he named **salivary amylase** and **pancreatic amylase**. **Lipase** digests **fat**. A tablet from outside is not a substitute for the body making its own enzymes |
| **Collagen** | A **structural** protein. It forms **bones and muscles** |

What a given protein does is decided by its **three-dimensional structure**. Three things build that structure: the **total number** of amino acids, the **amino-acid sequence** (the order), and the **complex interactions** among those amino acids. The design is pre-decided in the DNA, and the messenger RNA carries the precise version: how many, which one first, which one second. A slight change can alter the function and become a **genetic disease**, because a mutation in the DNA alters the messenger RNA, so a different amino acid sits in a different position.

## 6. Central dogma, and the one virus that runs it backwards (ST-13-06)

The master plan is the **genome**, the **DNA**. The instructions for these proteins sit in the DNA as **genes**, or the genetic code. To use them, the DNA makes an intermediate, **messenger ribonucleic acid (mRNA)**. Making that messenger is **transcription**. Turning it into protein is **translation**. The flow is **unidirectional**: DNA, then mRNA, then protein. That flow is the **central dogma**.

mRNA is the **template** of the protein, the precise design. DNA is the master copy. He compared DNA to a constitution and the messenger to the text that is actually carried out. One messenger is the recipe for haemoglobin. Another is the recipe for a different protein. The number and the order of amino acids differ.

Every living system follows it: **bacteria, fungi, plants, animals**. For all of them the genetic material is DNA.

Inside the cell, transcription happens in the **nucleus**, on the double-stranded DNA, and produces a **single-stranded** mRNA. The messenger leaves through **pores** in the nuclear membrane into the **cytoplasm**, the fluid matrix of the cell. There it meets the **ribosome**, the protein factory. The factory needs a design, and the design arrives as mRNA. The ribosome decodes it into an amino-acid chain, **one by one**. The chain then **folds** into the three-dimensional protein, and that shape decides whether the protein will be haemoglobin, an antibody, or an enzyme.

He read a short passage and asked them to keep two words. A large part of the DNA sequence **encodes proteins**, which carry out most of the functional tasks in all **extant** organisms. **Extant means surviving.** The information is made available by transcription, and the messenger is decoded **at the ribosome** into the amino-acid sequence. He placed this simplest statement in **1970**. The name on the audio was “Craig”. That is the Crick statement of the central dogma. Do not learn a scientist named Craig.

A **virus** is **acellular**. It has **no ribosome** of its own. Any of this happens **inside a living host cell**, and the virus uses the **host ribosome** to make protein.

- A **DNA virus** still goes messenger RNA, then protein. Transcription, then translation, inside the host.
- An **RNA virus** arrives with a **ready-made mRNA**. SARS-CoV-2 is the example. It enters the host and starts making protein. That is why these viruses spread fast.
- The **exception** is a **retrovirus**. The example is **HIV**, the human immunodeficiency virus, an RNA virus. Its RNA produces **viral DNA**, using its own enzyme, **reverse transcriptase**. That step is **reverse transcription**. After that the path is the ordinary one: DNA, then RNA, then protein. The flow does not obey the central dogma at the first step. When a question says retrovirus, the context he wants is HIV and this reverse step.

## 7. IceCube, and the 2026 physics prize (ST-13-07)

He closed on the day’s newspaper. The **Nobel Prize in Physics, 2026**. He told them to read at least three papers. What he dictated is the overview, so the papers can be read against it.

The **IceCube Neutrino Observatory** is at the **South Pole, Antarctica**. It is a **gigantic particle detector**, buried **deep inside the Antarctic ice**. The ice sheet is the instrument. He said that natural ice, in that form, is not available everywhere in Antarctica. The one-line contrast with India’s observatory in Tamil Nadu stays the atoms lecture. This class is the prize, and what the ice is doing.

**Neutrinos** are **electrically neutral**. Their mass is **negligible, and it is not zero**. He said non-zero mass, not massless. He did not give a number. A figure on the sheet is not a mass to learn. They were first called **ghost particles** because they pass through the Earth and through the body **without a trace**, and they interact with matter **extremely rarely**.

They are not bent. They do not interact with even a **strong magnetic field**, so they pass **undeflected**. **In contrast to electromagnetic waves such as visible light, the direction from which a neutrino arrives points directly back to its source.**

Two sources.

- **Solar neutrinos.** **Billions** of them pass through the Earth, and through a person, **every second**. They are produced by **nuclear fusion** in the Sun. The sheet can be read as if solar neutrinos do not come from fusion. The speech says they do.
- **Cosmic events in deep space**, inside the Milky Way and **beyond** it. He named **exploding stars**, **black holes**, and **neutron stars**. His picture of a neutron star: **1 to 2 times** the mass of the Sun, condensed into **20 to 25 kilometres**. His picture of a black hole: **20, 30, or 40 times** the mass of the Sun, as examples of “even more massive”, not one official mass. Stars, like the Sun, produce their energy by nuclear fusion. Detecting the neutrino is how he said one tells a solar neutrino from a neutron-star neutrino from a distant black-hole neutrino. They are messengers. They open distant galaxies, and they reveal **previously unknown** phenomena.

When a neutrino meets the ice it produces **blue light**, **Cherenkov radiation**. The audio said “Kern-Cohr”. The detector looks for **faint light flashes** made when these nearly massless, rarely interacting neutrinos **collide with the ice**. IceCube has shown that **high-energy neutrinos arrive from both within the Milky Way and from beyond it**. That is a new way to trace exploding stars, and activity around black holes and neutron stars.

**Francis Halzen**, a **Belgian-American** particle physicist, **received the 2026 Nobel Prize in Physics** for creating an observatory to detect some of the universe’s most elusive particles, the high-energy neutrinos, and for trying to establish how they are made. He realised that **ice at the South Pole** could track them. He **pioneered** IceCube. He named no other laureate. Do not add one.
