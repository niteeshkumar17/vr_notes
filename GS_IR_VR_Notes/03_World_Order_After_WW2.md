## 1. Superpowers, military blocs, capitalism vs communism (IR-03-01)

After WW2, two **superpowers**: **United States of America (USA)** and **Union of Soviet Socialist Republics (USSR) / Soviet Union**.

**Superpower** = extraordinarily powerful: the rest of the planet **cannot** achieve **Balance of Power (BOP)** against it. Only **another superpower** can counterbalance. Same ambition (dominate the world) → rivalry in **two** dimensions.

### Military

**Arms race** (nuclear tests: India **twice** — **1974** and **1998**; the two superpowers did **hundreds**). Two alliances:

| | **North Atlantic Treaty Organization (NATO)** | **Warsaw Pact** |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Led by | USA | USSR |
| Geography of the name | Friends in **North America** + **West Europe** = rim of the **North Atlantic** | Signed in the city of **Warsaw**; mostly **East Europe** |
| Guarantee | US security against USSR | Soviet security against US / NATO |

### Ideological

USA / NATO = **capitalism**. USSR / Warsaw = **communism**. They **cannot coexist**: communism **blames capitalism** for society’s problems.

| Capitalism | Communism (Karl Marx → **Marxism**) |
|:---|:---|
| **Free market** — no inspector / licence raj; **demand and supply** | Root problem = **class struggle** (economic inequality) |
| Example policies: **Liberalisation, Privatisation, Globalisation (LPG)** | **Abolish** the right to own **private property**; only **Public Sector Undertakings (PSUs)** |
| Rich / **capitalist** class = **ownership of property** (land, bank balance, factories — Tata / Ambani / Adani as class examples) | Poor / **working class** = one asset, **labour** |
| | Goal: **egalitarian / classless** society |

Capitalists will not give up property → a **revolution** is required. Two schools:

1. **Peaceful** — **Communist Party of India (CPI)** contests elections (Lok Sabha / Rajya Sabha; governments in **Kerala**, earlier **West Bengal**, **Tripura**; **United Progressive Alliance (UPA)** partners under Manmohan Singh). Legal.  
2. **Violent** — **Mao Zedong**, Chinese revolution **1949**: *power flows from the barrel of the gun.* Followers = **Maoists**. **Communist Party of India (Maoist)** is **banned**. Movement started at **Naxalbari** → **Naxalite**. **Central Reserve Police Force (CRPF)** fights it. Sandhu: “on the verge of eradicating.” **IS-01 lock stays: too early to call Naxal-free.** Sheet “CPI(M) banned” = **Maoist**, not CPI(Marxist).

**Bipolar** = two poles (US and USSR). Whole world split: Western bloc vs Eastern bloc.

## 2. Three worlds, NAM, Cold War, MAD, proxy (IR-03-02)

| World | Who | Ideology / policy |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **First** | Rich / developed; friends of USA | Capitalism |
| **Second** | Friends of USSR | Communism |
| **Third** | **Post-colonial** poor / developing states | **Non-alignment** — join **neither NATO nor Warsaw**; spend scarce resources on **growth**, not the superpower fight |

Not **tripolar**: the third “pole” was **powerless**.

**Non-Aligned Movement (NAM):** post-colonial states’ movement. **Jawaharlal Nehru** = one of the **founding fathers**. India encouraged other newly free states to stay out of the two camps.

**Cold War (1945–1991):** they hated each other, ran alliances and an arms race, but **never a direct (hot) war**. Reason: **nuclear deterrence** (*deter* = frighten). Supported by **realism**: two methods of **national security** = **BOP** (Lecture 1) + **nuclear deterrence**.

**Nuclear Weapon State (NWS) = 9:** USA, United Kingdom (UK), France, Russia, China (**Permanent Five (P-5)**) **+ India, Pakistan, Israel, North Korea**. Everyone else = **Non-Nuclear Weapon State (NNWS)**.

**Nuclear war** = **NWS vs NWS**. **Not** nuclear war: Russia–Ukraine; US–Iran; Hiroshima–Nagasaki (use of a bomb, Japan had **no** bomb). A nuclear war has **never** happened. Outcome = **Mutually Assured Destruction (MAD)** — all lose, **zero** winners. States do **cost–benefit analysis** (rational actors) → they do not fight it. Today’s weapons are **thousands of times** stronger than the **experimental** Hiroshima bombs (class: Oppenheimer). **No defence** except a deep bunker (class: **200–300 feet**, stay **40–50 years**) — not a GS number to invent beyond class.

**Still valid:** US–Russia, US–China, **India–Pakistan**, India–China cannot fight a **direct** war. India–Pakistan wars were **before** both had bombs. Class: if India had bombs in **1962**, China would not have attacked. **Iran** wants a bomb for the same deterrent; US does not want Iran to have it.

**Proxy / indirect war:** an NWS cannot fight another NWS → a friend (usually **NNWS**) fights **on its behalf** (money, weapons, intel). Cold War examples: **Korea** (South = US proxy, North = Soviet); **Vietnam** (North vs South); **Afghanistan 1979**.

**Today:** Pakistan’s proxies against India = **Lashkar-e-Taiba (LeT)** and **Jaish-e-Mohammed (JeM)**. **Operation Sindoor** = destroy **proxy infrastructure** (terrorist camps), **not** a full-scale war (MAD). Class: camps at **nine** locations; operation in **28–29 minutes**.

**Answer drop:** *Cold War stayed cold because of MAD, not because the UN “kept peace.” Superpowers fought through proxies. NAM was the powerless third world opting out.*

## 3. Why the USSR broke, 1991 (IR-03-03)

**1991:** USSR **disintegrates** into **15** countries. **Russia** = successor (capital **Moscow**; inherits Soviet nuclear weapons). **Warsaw Pact dissolved**. **USA won** the Cold War.

Class names (not a complete 15): Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistan, Turkmenistan, Uzbekistan, Azerbaijan, Armenia, Belarus, Ukraine, Russia.

| External | Internal |
|:---|:---|
| Power tussle with USA; **expensive arms race**; had to **defend Warsaw** members | **Weak economy** = **failed communism** — no incentive if you **cannot own property**; **over-centralisation of power** (sheet) |

Class equality line: Constitution wants **equal opportunity**, not a mechanical society with **no reward for merit**. After 1991, even the 15 successor states **adopted capitalism**; communist parties became **unpopular**.

## 4. NPT, NSG, IAEA — India 2008 waiver (IR-03-04)

**Treaty on the Non-Proliferation of Nuclear Weapons (NPT):** opened **1968**, in force **1970**. Cut-off **1 January 1967**: bombs **before** that date = recognised **NWS** = **P-5**. Restrictions sit on **NNWS**.

**Five non-parties (class):** **India, Pakistan, Israel, North Korea, South Sudan**. Four of those five have bombs (South Sudan does not). India did **not** sign: NPT is **discriminatory** — nuclear **haves and have-nots**; lets P-5 **dominate**.

**Nuclear Suppliers Group (NSG)** = **London Club** — nuclear **technology + fuel** holders; will not share with a country that has not accepted NPT (civilian use only). **International Atomic Energy Agency (IAEA)** checks no **military misuse**; works **with** NSG. Class: Iran took NSG fuel after accepting NPT; IAEA later reported problems (detail in a later US/Iran class).

**2008:** **USA** helped India get an **NSG and IAEA waiver** — **exception only for India** so NSG members **can** share civilian nuclear technology with India **without** NPT. Full US-lecture later.

## 5. Post–Cold War: unipolar LPG, then the dark side (IR-03-05)

**1991 onward = unipolar** = **US hegemony**. Economics: **LPG** → **open economy**. **Globalisation** = **free trade / trade without barriers**. India: **1991** reforms.

**Non-state actors** rise with integration:

- **Multinational Corporations (MNCs)** — **Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR)**  
- **Non-Governmental Organisations (NGOs)** — e.g. **Amnesty** (human rights)  
- **International Monetary Fund (IMF) / World Bank (WB) / World Trade Organization (WTO)** — class: **Washington HQ** (IMF/WB)  
- **Transnational Terrorist Networks (TTNs)** — **Al-Qaeda**; **Osama bin Laden** from **Afghanistan**; **11 September 2001 (9/11)** — sheet **~3,000** Americans died. **JeM / LeT** sit here as Pakistan-based networks.

**Dark side of globalisation:** **illegal arms and drugs smuggling** because economies are **integrated**.

**Economic interdependence** → **multilateral** approach to global problems.

**2008 global financial crisis:** US economy / stock markets crash → **setback** to free trade / globalisation / liberalisation → **protectionism** (**tariff barriers** to **protect domestic industry**). Class: after 2008, more countries (including “manufacturing in India” talk) turn protectionist = **crisis of globalisation**.

## 6. Trump, BRICS, China, new Cold War, Indo-Pacific (IR-03-06)

**January 2017 — Donald Trump:** US policy **protectionist** (national interest) → **de-globalisation**. Later **Biden**, then Trump again; class treats the **protectionist turn** as lasting. **Make America Great Again (MAGA)** — red cap; class is **sceptical** the tariffs will “bring manufacturing home.” Trump vs India: wants **lower Indian tariffs** so US firms need not manufacture **in India**. Class: threat of **100% tariff** if BRICS even **thinks** de-dollarisation.

**Decline of the West / rise of the rest** → **multipolar**. **Brazil, Russia, India, China, South Africa (BRICS)** = emerging economies; **beneficiaries** of globalisation (they **support** it). **BRICS Plus** — class: **Indonesia** has joined. US **MNCs** do **offshore manufacturing** in BRICS (India, China, South Africa, Brazil, Indonesia) → US **loses manufacturing** → **loses hegemony**. Angry US workers → Trump.

**US economic hegemony (three legs class):** (1) **highest Gross Domestic Product (GDP)** — class **~$30 trillion**; (2) **US dollar** as trade/oil **anchor** (America cannot have a classic **Balance of Payments (BOP)** crisis the way others can); (3) **IMF / World Bank**.

**BRICS counters:** grow GDP; **de-dollarisation** (yuan, dirham; talk of a BRICS currency); **New Development Bank (NDB)** as alternative to IMF/WB.
