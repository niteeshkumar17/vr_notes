## 1. Commissioner Stays Jauhar University Demolition Order

- **Jauhar University** — Located in **Jauhar Nagar** in the **Rampur district** of **Uttar Pradesh**.

### Demolition Law in India

- Regulated by the **National Building Code (NBC)** & **IS 4130** safety code.
- **IS 4130** — Issued by the **Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS)**.

### Core Requirements of IS 4130

| Requirement | Description |
|-------------|-------------|
| **I) Pre-Demolition Survey** | Check before work |
| **II) Danger Signage & Barricading** | Safety warnings and barriers |
| **III) Sequential Dismantling** | Controlled, step-by-step demolition |
| **IV) Strict Exclusion** | Code applies to standard structures (homes, offices, factories). It **excludes** specialized or high-hazard demolitions such as nuclear plants, dams, or underwater structures. |

### Mandatory Show-Cause Notice

- **Minimum 15 days** must be given.

### Implementation & Documentation

- **Pre-demolition Audit:** Before heavy machinery moves in, authorities draft an inspection report **witnessed & signed by two independent local residents (Panchas)**.
- **Video Recording:** The entire demolition process must be **continuously video recorded**.
- **Exemptions:** This rigorous procedure is bypassed only if an unauthorized structure is actively blocking **public roads, footpaths, railway tracks, or water bodies**.

## 2. Madras HC Strikes Down Orders Granting Govt. Jobs to Kin of Karur Stampede Victims

- **Karur** — A prominent industrial city of the Karur district located in the **south-central part of Tamil Nadu**.
- Struck down for **violation of Articles 14 & 16** of the Constitution.
- **State's argument:** Granted jobs in exercise of its executive powers under **Article 162** of the Constitution.

### Article 14 — Right to Equality

Guarantees the **Right to Equality** to every person within the territory of India.

#### Two Core Pillars of Article 14

| Pillar | Source | Key Detail |
|--------|--------|------------|
| **Equality Before the Law** | Borrowed from **British common law** tradition | **Exception:** Foreign diplomats, President of India, State Governors enjoy certain constitutional immunities from criminal proceedings while in office. |
| **Equal Protection of the Laws** | Borrowed from **Section 1 of the 14th Amendment of the US Constitution** | **Meaning:** Equals must be treated equally, but unequals cannot be treated equally. **Impact:** Allows the State to make affirmative action policies, special laws, or reservations to uplift historically disadvantaged sections (Women, SCs, STs). |

### Article 16 — Equality of Opportunity in Public Employment

Guarantees **equality of opportunity** in matters of public employment. Ensures every citizen has an equal chance to apply for & hold office or jobs under the State.

#### Clause-by-Clause Breakdown

| Clause | Provision |
|--------|-----------|
| **16(1)** | Assures equal opportunity for all citizens in State-funded employment, appointments, or offices. |
| **16(2)** | Strictly prohibits discrimination in public employment based on **seven specific grounds**: religion, race, caste, sex, descent, place of birth, residence, or any of them. |
| **16(3)** *(Exception)* | Allows Parliament to pass laws making **residence a mandatory requirement** for certain government jobs in specific States or Union Territories (to protect local employment). |
| **16(4)** *(Reservation Provision)* | Empowers the State to make **reservations in appointments or posts** for any "backward class of citizens" if the State believes they are not adequately represented in public services. |
| **16(4A)** | Allows the State to provide reservation for **SCs & STs in promotions** with consequential seniority. |
| **16(4B)** *(50% Ceiling not counted)* | Permits **carry-forward vacancies** — unfilled reserved seats from one year can be carried over to the next without being bound by the general 50% ceiling limit on reservation for that specific year. |
| **16(5)** | Allows laws to mandate that an incumbent of an office connected with a **religious institution** must follow that specific religion. |
| **16(6)** | Permits up to a **10% reservation** in public employment for the **Economically Weaker Sections (EWS)** of citizens not covered under SC, ST, or OBC reservation quotas. |

### Article 162 — Executive Power of a State

Defines the **extent of the executive power** of a State.

#### Key Legal Principles

| Principle | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Co-extensive Power** | The State government can run administration, implement policies & issue executive orders on any subjects listed under the **State List** (public order, police, public health, sanitation, etc.). |
| **Concurrent List Caveat** | If a subject falls under the **Concurrent List**, the State's executive power is strictly **subordinate to the Centre**. |
| **Action without Prior Legislation** | In *Ram Jawaya Kapur vs State of Punjab*, the Supreme Court ruled that the State executive doesn't have to wait for an active law to be passed before taking action — as long as the State has the **power to make a law** on the topic. |

## 4. Kerala Names Ashutosh Dash as Its Expert on Dam Panel

### Mullaperiyar Dam

- **Constructed:** 1887–1895 | **Type:** Masonry gravity dam.
- **Location:** **Periyar River** in the **Idukki district** of **Kerala**.

### Core Dispute: Safety vs. Supply

| State | Position |
|-------|----------|
| **Kerala** | Wants to decommission old dam and build a new one. |
| **Tamil Nadu** | Opposes new dam — fears losing control over water. |

- Currently **Tamil Nadu controls the dam** — the dam area of **8,000 acres** was leased to the **Madras Presidency (British India)** for **999 years**.

### Supreme Court Interventions

| Year | Verdict / Action |
|------|-----------------|
| **2006** | SC allowed Tamil Nadu to **raise water level from 136 ft to 142 ft**. **Retaliation:** Kerala passed the *Kerala Irrigation & Water Conservation (Amendment) Act*, legally declaring the dam "endangered" & restricted the water level back to **136 ft**. |
| **2014** | A Constitution Bench of SC **struck down Kerala's 2006 law as unconstitutional**. Court found the dam was safe and raised the level to **142 ft**. |
| **Supervisory Committee** | To manage ongoing tension & oversee dam safety, the SC established a **permanent Supervisory Committee**. |
