## Topic 1: For newborns, the answer is hospital plus home (Abhay Bang / SEARCH)

> **Micro-Topic ID:** `CA-260904-01`  
> **GS Paper:** **GS-II** (health, social sector) + **GS-I** (society)

### 1. What is in the news
Physician **Abhay Bang** (founder, **Society for Education, Action and Research in Community Health (SEARCH)**, Gadchiroli) argues that newborn deaths have shifted from homes into **clusters in government hospitals** — fires, oxygen failure, overcrowding — because **Janani Suraksha Yojana (JSY)**-era institutional delivery succeeded without matching **safety and staff**.

### 2. Numbers in the clip
- Institutional deliveries: **39% (2005–06) → 90% (2023–24)**
- Institutional births: **109 lakh (2005) → 194 lakh (2024–25)**
- Public **Special Newborn Care Unit (SNCU)** admissions: **11.3 lakh (2021–22) → 14.45 lakh (2023–24)** (**+28%**)
- Named hospital tragedies in the piece include **Gorakhpur / Baba Raghav Das (BRD) Medical College (2017)**, **Ahmedabad (2017)**, **Kota JK Lon (2020)**, fire cases at **Bhandara (2021)**, **Jhansi (2025)**, **Amravati (24 August 2026)**

### 3. Why units fail (clip)
Changing **case mix** (more premature / low-birth-weight babies reaching hospital); **infrastructure** (oxygen, electrical fires); poor **nurse-to-baby** ratios; weak **infection prevention**.

### 4. Gadchiroli / Home-Based Newborn Care (HBNC)
SEARCH trial: **62.2%** fall in rural **neonatal mortality** (*The Lancet*, **1999**). **97%** of low-birth-weight / preterm babies **above 1,800 g and 34 weeks** managed at home with very low case-fatality.

### 5. Optimum strategy (clip)
1. **Decongest** SNCUs — stable newborns via **HBNC** through India’s **~8 lakh Accredited Social Health Activists (ASHAs)**  
2. **Strengthen** facilities (staff, equipment, backup power)  
3. **Intrinsic safety** — fire detection / suppression + **independent safety audits**

### 6. One-liner
**JSY filled the hospitals; HBNC + safer SNCUs** must now share the load — **hospital plus home**.

## Topic 2: ‘Supersized’ El Niño to be strongest ever recorded — WMO

> **Micro-Topic ID:** `CA-260904-02`  
> **GS Paper:** **GS-I** (climatology) + **GS-III** (disaster / agriculture)

### 1. What is in the news
The **World Meteorological Organization (WMO)** (Geneva, Thursday) said this **El Niño** may become the **strongest since monitoring began** — “literally off the charts” if the path holds (**Celeste Saulo**, Secretary-General). First time WMO has stated **near-100%** confidence it will last through **February 2027**.

### 2. Mechanism (clip)
Periodic warming of **eastern Pacific** sea-surface temperature when **trade winds weaken**. Usual rhythm: every **2–7 years**, typically **9–12 months**. This one is being fed by **exceptionally warm** Pacific water and is expected to run **into 2027**.

### 3. Impacts (clip)
Shifts rainfall and temperature **worldwide**; **droughts** and **typhoons**; **intense rains and droughts** across **Latin America**. WMO is stepping up **preparedness and early warning**.

Class hook (do not restudy as a new lecture): El Niño often **weakens the Indian monsoon** — `05_Agriculture_Society_Climate_and_Stubble.md` / `04_Agriculture_Importance_Trade_and_Commodities.md`.

### 4. One-liner
WMO: this El Niño is **near-certain through Feb 2027** and may be the **strongest on record**.
