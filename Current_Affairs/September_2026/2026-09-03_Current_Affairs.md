## Topic 1: UN charts course to manage global warming-limit breach (UNEP “overshoot, peak, decline”)

> **Micro-Topic ID:** `CA-260903-01`  
> **GS Paper:** **GS-III** (Environment + Climate Change)

### 1. What is in the news
UN Environment Programme (UNEP) has warned that a possible breach of the **1.5°C** global warming limit may become unavoidable and could trigger **irreversible or no-longer-avoidable ecological consequences**.

UNEP (in a report released this week) sets out a detailed pathway often described as **“overshoot, peak and decline”**: even if temperature overshoots 1.5°C, the world should return **back within the accepted limit by 2100**.

The UNEP report frames an “overshoot” scenario as a realistic planning pathway when avoidance of warming is no longer possible.

### 2. Key climate numbers (as per clip)
- Global warming at the current trajectory is projected to rise to about **1.8°C** in the report’s scenario where **all national climate plans** are delivered.
- UNEP notes that **current policies** point towards about **2.6°C by 2100**.

### 3. Why UPSC cares
- The idea is not just a “doom forecast”, but a policy framing: what actions should states plan for if overshoot is already likely.
- Fits Prelims concepts around mitigation pathways, temperature goals, and UN climate architecture (planning targets).

### 4. One-liner
UNEP says **overshoot → peak → decline** is the remaining best planning pathway if 1.5°C avoidance is no longer assured.

## Topic 2: Formulate rules for fishing vessel transit to EEZ; SC directs TN

> **Micro-Topic ID:** `CA-260903-02`  
> **GS Paper:** **GS-II** (Judiciary, federalism) + **GS-III** (Maritime governance)

### 1. What is in the news
The **Supreme Court** directed **Tamil Nadu (TN)** to **formulate rules** for the **transit of fishing vessels to the Exclusive Economic Zone (EEZ)**.

The dispute in the case is about **cooperative federalism**—how responsibilities are shared between the Union and States for regulating purse-seine fishing vessels and ensuring legal transit through maritime zones.

### 2. Context from the clip (what TN was told)
- The court referred to the need for a **clear regulatory framework** covering transit beyond coastal waters (towards the **EEZ**).
- TN is stated to have issued/been covered by **Marine Fishing Regulation Rules, 2020**, but the court still wanted rules ensuring smooth and compliant vessel transit to the EEZ.

### 3. Why UPSC cares
- Federal governance + maritime regulation: states’ coastal responsibilities vs Union/EEZ regulatory approach.
- Often asked as “what does SC direct + what is the regulatory gap it points to”.

### 4. One-liner
SC asked TN to make the **EEZ transit rules** for fishing vessels, strengthening Union-State coordination in maritime regulation.

## Topic 3: Japan Credit Rating Agency upgrade lifts India’s credit rating a notch on “high growth”

> **Micro-Topic ID:** `CA-260903-03`  
> **GS Paper:** **GS-III** (Economy + Financial markets)

### 1. What is in the news
The **Japan Credit Rating Agency (JCR)** upgraded India’s credit rating by **one notch**, moving it from **A-** to **BBB+**.

The clip also mentions that the upgrade is backed by:
- India’s **high-growth-oriented policies** and improved financial system strength, and
- government steps conducive to **productivity/economic development**, including **digital public infrastructure** and **GST implementation**.

### 2. Why the upgrade matters (as per clip logic)
A higher rating means India can borrow at **lower costs**, which reduces the **interest burden on the exchequer**.

### 3. One-liner
JCR’s upgrade (A- → BBB+) supports the view that India’s growth and financial-policy environment is improving.
