## Topic 2: India–Japan defence — UNICORN and Veer Guardian

> **Micro-Topic ID:** `CA-260909-02`  
> **GS Paper:** **GS-II** (IR, Indo-Pacific) + **GS-III** (defence)

### 1. What is in the news
Op-ed (**Harsh V. Pant** / **Pratnashree Basu**, Observer Research Foundation). Japan’s Defence Minister **Shinjiro Koizumi** visited in **August 2026**. Clip: the pair is moving from a decade of institutions (**2+2**, exercises, logistics) to **operational** capability.

Both worry about coercive attempts to change the **Indo-Pacific** status quo. **Maritime Domain Awareness (MDA)** / information-sharing: Japan’s watch is the **East China Sea** and waters around **Taiwan**; India’s geography sits on the **Indian Ocean**.

Class Indo-Pacific definition (do not restudy as a new lecture): **Indian Ocean + Pacific + surrounding land** — `GS_IR_VR_Notes/03_World_Order_After_WW2.md`.

### 2. Hardware lock
**UNICORN** = **Unified Complex Radio Antenna** — Japan’s integrated communications antenna. **Memorandum of Understanding (MoU), November 2024:** **Bharat Electronics Limited (BEL)** and Japanese authorities to **co-develop UNICORN masts**. Clip also: **Make in India** production and **ship-repair** facilities.

### 3. Exercise lock
Japanese **fighter aircraft** in **Veer Guardian** in India, **9–22 September 2026** — clip: **first time**. Sides want harder bilateral drills, **unmanned** systems, and short-notice exercises.

**July 2026 Annual Summit** (clip): economic security, critical technologies, resilient supply chains — partnership **not only** a China-centric agenda.

### 4. One-liner
**UNICORN masts = BEL + Japan, MoU November 2024.** **Veer Guardian 9–22 Sep 2026** = first Japanese fighters in that India exercise.
