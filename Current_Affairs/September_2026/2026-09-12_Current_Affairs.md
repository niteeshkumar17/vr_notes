## Topic 1: Delhi HC seeks Centre and Delhi government stand on constituting the Waqf Board

> **Micro-Topic ID:** `CA-260912-01`  
> **GS Paper:** **GS-II** (Centre–State, statutory bodies, rule of law, minority institutions)

### 1. What is in the news
The **Delhi High Court** has asked the **Centre** and the **Delhi government** for their stand on a petition seeking **completion of the statutory process** to **constitute** the **Delhi Waqf Board**.

**Bench:** Justice **Anish Dayal**. **Notice** issued **8 September**. Next listing: **10 December**.

**Petition:** **Mohd. Shahid**; counsel **Rizwan Ahmad**.

### 2. What the clip locks

| Fact | Clip |
|:---|:---|
| Last elected / sitting board | Tenure **expired 23 August 2023**. **No fresh board** after that. |
| Stop-gap | Delhi government appointed an **Administrator** on **10 January 2024**. |
| 2025 law | Petitioner: **Parliament retained the obligation to constitute Waqf Boards** despite **amendments made in 2025**. (Clip does **not** name the amending Act or its sections — do not invent.) |
| Harm alleged | Continued absence **exposed waqf properties** to **encroachment**, **unauthorised occupation**, and **deterioration**. |
| Legal frame (plea) | Failure = **arbitrary executive inaction**; **violates the rule of law**. |

This is **not** the medieval *waqf*-as-endowment lock on `GS_History_VR_Notes/Medieval_India.md` (Alauddin taxed waqf). Today is a **living statutory board** that has not been reconstituted.

### 3. One-liner
Delhi Waqf Board tenure ended **23 Aug 2023**; **Administrator since 10 Jan 2024**; HC (Anish Dayal) wants Centre + Delhi stand; petitioner says **2025 amendments still require Boards to be constituted**. List **10 Dec**.

<span style="color: #e53e3e;">**Prelims trap:** Administrator **≠** a reconstituted Board. **2025 amendments did not drop** the duty to constitute Boards (plea). Do not mix with Sultanate *waqf*.</span>
