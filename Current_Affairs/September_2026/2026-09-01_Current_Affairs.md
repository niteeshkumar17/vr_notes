## Topic 1: INS Nipun — Second Nistar-class Diving Support Vessel

> **Micro-Topic ID:** `CA-260901-01`  
> **GS Paper:** **GS-III** (Defence, indigenisation, maritime capacity)

### 1. What is in the news
The Indian Navy commissioned **INS Nipun** on **Monday, 31 August 2026** at the **Naval Dockyard, Mumbai**. Hull number **A17**.

It is the **second Diving Support Vessel (DSV)** of the **Nistar class**. The ceremony was presided over by Chief of the Naval Staff **Admiral Krishna Swaminathan**.

The Ministry of Defence called it a specialist platform, **indigenously designed and built** by **Hindustan Shipyard Limited (HSL), Visakhapatnam** — a step toward **Atmanirbhar** defence shipbuilding.

### 2. Roles
* Deep-sea diving
* Underwater intervention
* Submarine rescue
