## Topic 1: SC seeks clarity on FSSAI front-of-pack warning labels

> **Micro-Topic ID:** `CA-260911-01`  
> **GS Paper:** **GS-II** (judiciary, public health regulation) + **GS-III** (food safety, packaged food)

### 1. What is in the news
The **Supreme Court** on **Thursday (10 September 2026)** questioned the **Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI)** on how it would decide that a packaged food is **“high”** in **sugar, salt or fat** for its proposed **front-of-pack warning labels (FoPL)**. Food safety, the Bench said, is a cause of **“national interest.”**

**Bench:** Justices **J.B. Pardiwala** and **K. Vinod Chandran**.

**Matter:** public-interest litigation by non-profits (**3S** and **Our Health Society**; advocate **Rajiv Shankar Dvivedi** in the same day’s Hindu) seeking **mandatory** front-of-pack warnings on packaged foods high in sugar, salt and saturated fats.

**Appearing for Centre + FSSAI:** **Additional Solicitor-General (ASG) Brijender Chahar**.

### 2. What FSSAI proposed (affidavit)

| Lock | Class / clip line |
|:---|:---|
| Shape / colour | **Red hexagonal** warning on the **front** of the pack |
| Words | **“HIGH FAT” / “HIGH SUGAR” / “HIGH SALT”** (and, in the proposal, **“HIGHLY SWEETENED BEVERAGE”** where it applies) |
| Trigger (first design) | Product **high in two or more** nutrients of concern — **fat, sugar or salt** |
| Thresholds | **Dietary Guidelines for Indians, 2024**, issued by the **Indian Council of Medical Research (ICMR) – National Institute of Nutrition (NIN)** |
| Last month | FSSAI proposed **prominent red** warning labels — the paper calls this a **regulatory U-turn** |

The Court asked the obvious Prelims trap: if you say **high in salt / high in sugar / high in sodium**, **have you laid down guidelines** for the cut-off? It also asked whether FSSAI was adhering to the **ICMR–NIN** guidelines.

Justice Pardiwala (clip): **“We are concerned with the health of people, particularly growing children.”** Take the Court’s orders **seriously**. Homework and study have been done; **cooperation** is expected in a **national-interest** cause.

### 3. Same day’s written order (same reporter — do not skip)

After the hearing the Bench’s order (Thursday night) put a **two-phase** FoPL rollout under the scanner.

- **Phase 1 (FSSAI design):** warning if **two or more** nutrients of concern are high.  
- **Phase 2:** extend to products high in **even one**.  
- FSSAI’s reason: time for consumers to adapt and for industry to **reformulate**.  
- **SC:** consumer acceptability / industry time **cannot** leave Phase 2 **indefinitely postponed**. Need a **reasonable, scientifically justified, clearly defined** timeline between the two phases.

**Also in the order (Hindu):**

- Consider an alternative: start with products at the **highest** levels of nutrients of concern, then **progressively lower** thresholds.  
- Why wait for **two** nutrients when **one** excess nutrient can harm?  
- **Nutritional literacy in schools** — children are **vulnerable** to impulse / uninformed diet choices; teach how to read labels.  
- **Insufficient information** on hexagon **size and placement**. Word-only labels assume literacy; ideally **words + pictorial** nutrients so the label works across languages and ages.  
- Reducing salt/sugar/fat must not push makers into more **artificial preservatives / emulsifiers** — keep that danger in mind.  
- **ASG Chahar** told the Court the government was **open** to warnings even when **one** nutrient is high (the U-turn vs two-or-more).  
- FSSAI to file an **affidavit in 10 days**. Next hearing **28 September 2026**.

### 4. One-liner
**FoPL = red hexagon on the pack.** First design = **two or more** of fat/sugar/salt, cut-offs from **ICMR–NIN Dietary Guidelines 2024**. SC (Pardiwala–Vinod Chandran): **what is “high”?** Phase 2 needs a **real timeline**. Affidavit **10 days**; list **28 Sep**.
