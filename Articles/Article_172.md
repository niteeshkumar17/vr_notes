# Article 172 — Duration of State Legislatures

<details>
<summary>Show Answer</summary>

## Text (Simplified)

1. **Legislative Assembly (Vidhan Sabha):** Every Legislative Assembly of every State, unless sooner dissolved, shall continue for **five years** from the date appointed for its first meeting and no longer and the expiration of the said period of 5 years shall operate as a dissolution of the Assembly:
   * **Proviso (National Emergency):** While a Proclamation of Emergency (Article 352) is in operation, the said period may be extended by Parliament by law for a period not exceeding **one year at a time** and not extending beyond **six months** after the Proclamation has ceased to operate.
2. **Legislative Council (Vidhan Parishad):** The Legislative Council of a State shall **not be subject to dissolution**, but as nearly as possible **one-third of the members** thereof shall retire as soon as may be on the expiration of every second year in accordance with the provisions made by Parliament by law.

## Key Points for UPSC
* **Exact Mirror of Article 83 at State Level:** Legislative Assembly has a 5-year tenure; Legislative Council (in bicameral states) is a permanent body with 1/3rd members retiring every 2 years.
* **Premature Dissolution:** The Governor has the power to dissolve the Legislative Assembly under [Article 174(2)(b)](file:///c:/Users/nitee/OneDrive/Desktop/UPSE_Syllabus/Articles/Article_174.md), normally on the aid and advice of the Chief Minister/Council of Ministers.
* **Extension Mechanism:** Note that only **Parliament by law** (not the State Legislature itself) can extend the tenure of a State Legislative Assembly during a National Emergency under Article 352.
* <span style="color: #e53e3e;">**One Nation One Election (ONOE) Implication:** Any attempt to synchronise State Assemblies with Lok Sabha requires amending Article 172(1) — introducing provisions for early dissolution of ongoing terms or truncated midterm assemblies serving only the remainder of the 5-year national cycle. Requires ratification by &ge;50% of State Legislatures under Article 368(2) proviso.</span>
