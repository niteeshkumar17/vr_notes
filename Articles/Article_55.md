# Article 55: Manner of Election of President & Vote Values

> **Tags:** `[GS-2: Executive - President of India, Part V Chapter 1]`
> **Last Updated:** 2026-08-17
> **Related Notes:** [Polity_Lec2_AbheyKumar_President.md](../GS_Polity_VR_Notes/Polity_Lec2_AbheyKumar_President.md)

### Active Recall Questions

<details><summary>What is the core principle governing Article 55?</summary>

- **Uniformity of representation:** Uniformity among different States as well as parity between the States as a whole and the Union.
- **System of Voting:** System of **Proportional Representation by means of Single Transferable Vote (PR-STV)** and voting by secret ballot.
</details>

<details><summary>What is the formula for calculating the value of the vote of an MLA?</summary>

$$\text{Value of 1 MLA Vote} = \frac{\text{Total Population of State}}{\text{Total Elected MLAs}} \div 1000$$

- If remainder is $\ge 500$, add 1 to the quotient.
- **Highest MLA vote value:** Uttar Pradesh (208).
- **Lowest MLA vote value:** Sikkim (7).
</details>

<details><summary>What is the formula for calculating the value of the vote of an MP?</summary>

$$\text{Value of 1 MP Vote} = \frac{\text{Total Value of Votes of all MLAs of all States}}{\text{Total Number of Elected MPs (LS + RS)}}$$

- Current value of 1 MP vote = **700** (adjusted after J&K reorganisation).
</details>

<details><summary>Which census population is currently used for Article 55 vote value calculations?</summary>

- <span style="color: #e53e3e;">**1971 Census Freeze:** Under the 42nd Amendment (1976) extended by the **84th Constitutional Amendment Act, 2001**, the population of each State is frozen to the **1971 Census** until the relevant figures for the first census taken after the year **2026** have been published.</span>
- **Reason:** To prevent penalizing southern states that effectively controlled population growth under family planning policies.
</details>
