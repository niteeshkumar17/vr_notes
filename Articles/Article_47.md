# Article 47 of the Indian Constitution

**Part:** Part IV (Directive Principles of State Policy)

**Heading:** Duty of the State to raise the level of nutrition and the standard of living and to improve public health

**Key Provisions:**
*   The State shall regard the raising of the level of nutrition and the standard of living of its people and the improvement of public health as among its primary duties.
*   In particular, the State shall endeavour to bring about **prohibition of the consumption except for medicinal purposes of intoxicating drinks and of drugs which are injurious to health**.

**Relevance:** Forms the constitutional basis for anti-drug initiatives (like 'Nasha Mukt Yuva for Viksit Bharat'), liquor prohibition laws in various states, and public health policies addressing substance abuse.
