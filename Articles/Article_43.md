# Article 43 — DPSP: Living Wage, Conditions of Work & Participation of Workers

> **Part:** IV — Directive Principles of State Policy
> **Subject:** Living wage, etc., for workers

<details>
<summary>Show Answer</summary>

## Text

The State shall endeavour to secure, by suitable legislation or economic organisation or in any other way, to all workers, agricultural, industrial or otherwise, work, a living wage, conditions of work ensuring a decent standard of life and full enjoyment of leisure and social and cultural opportunities and, in particular, the State shall endeavour to promote cottage industries on an individual or co-operative basis in rural areas.

## Key Points
- Falls under **Socialistic Provisions** of the Constitution.
- Mandates **participation of workers in management of factories** — a direct socialistic provision.
- Workers' participation ensures: better working conditions, fair working hours, leisure, safety, security, insurance, fair wages, holidays, etc.
- All of these collectively represent a **minimum standard of living** → establishes a **welfare state**.
- Reflects **Gandhian Socialism** — empowering grassroot people, bottom-up approach, cottage industries.
- **Not justiciable** (being a DPSP) but fundamental in governance.

## Socialistic Significance
- When workers participate in management, they raise issues about their welfare → this is democratic socialism in action.
- The provision bridges the gap between the bourgeois (owners) and proletariat (workers) without abolishing private property.
- **<span style="color: #e53e3e;">Labour Codes & Modern Jurisprudence (Aug 2026):</span>** <span style="color: #e53e3e;">Article 43 and [Article 43A](Article_43A.md) provide the constitutional bedrock for India's 4 Labour Codes (Wage Code 2019, IRC 2020, Social Security Code 2020, OSH Code 2020).
