# Article 21 — Right to Life and Personal Liberty

<details>
<summary>Show Answer</summary>

* **Category**: Fundamental Rights (Part III)
* **Environment**: The Supreme Court has interpreted the Right to Life to encompass the right to a clean, pollution-free, and healthy environment.
* **Due Process**: SC cautioned against imposing rigid, statutory trial deadlines (e.g., in complex paper leak cases) because rushed trials compromise the right to a fair trial and defense preparation.
* **Right to Privacy vs. RTI**: <span style="color: #e53e3e;">Puttaswamy (2017) established Right to Privacy under Art 21 to protect citizens against State incursion. SC (Aug 2026) is hearing petitions challenging Sec 44(3) DPDP Act for turning privacy on its head by shielding State/public officers from RTI disclosure under Sec 8(1)(j) RTI Act.</span>
* **Nuclear Disasters & Absolute Liability**: <span style="color: #e53e3e;">In SHANTI Act 2025 hearing (Aug 2026), SC held that statutory liability caps cannot restrict Constitutional Courts under Art 32/226 from awarding suitable compensation against legal torts violating Right to Life (Art 21), upholding the Absolute Liability principle (M.C. Mehta).</span>
* **Biometric Surveillance & Proportionality**: <span style="color: #e53e3e;">In NEET protest FRS hearing (Aug 2026), SC held that police deployment of Facial Recognition Systems (FRS) and automated biometric surveillance at public demonstrations must satisfy the 4-prong Proportionality Test under Article 21 (Legality, Legitimate Aim, Suitability, Necessity/Least Intrusive Means, and Balancing) to prevent indiscriminate mass profiling.</span>
* **Dignified Livelihood & Minimum Wages**: <span style="color: #e53e3e;">In MGNREGA / VB-G RAM G Act proceedings (Aug 2026), SC considered the nexus between Article 21 (dignified existence / Olga Tellis livelihood) and statutory minimum wages, reaffirming that economic distress forcing labour below minimum wages violates dignity and transitions into Article 23 forced labour.</span>
* **Hazardous Mining & Ecological Buffer Rights**: <span style="color: #e53e3e;">In Meghalaya uranium mining resolution and Kaziranga ESZ debates (Aug 2026), Article 21's guarantee to a pollution-free and hazard-free environment was reinforced alongside Sixth Schedule tribal customary land sovereignty (SC Dimasa 2019) and ecological buffer protection under EPA 1986.</span>
