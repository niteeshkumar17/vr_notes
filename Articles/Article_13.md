# Article 13 of the Indian Constitution

**Part:** Part III (Fundamental Rights)

**Heading:** Laws inconsistent with or in derogation of the fundamental rights

<details>
<summary>Show Answer</summary>

**Key Provisions:**
*   Declares that all laws in force in the territory of India immediately before the commencement of the Constitution, in so far as they are inconsistent with the provisions of Part III, shall, to the extent of such inconsistency, be void.
*   The State shall not make any law which takes away or abridges the rights conferred by Part III and any law made in contravention of this clause shall, to the extent of the contravention, be void.
*   Provides the basis for **Judicial Review**. Laws can be declared *ultra vires* (beyond powers) if they violate Fundamental Rights.

**Relevance:** Frequently invoked when challenging legislative or executive actions (like the CAPF Act) that allegedly violate constitutional provisions or fundamental rights.
