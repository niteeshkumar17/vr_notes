# Article 2: Admission or Establishment of New States

> **Tags:** `[GS-2: Constitution - Part I Union & Territory, External Arrangement]`
> **Last Updated:** 2026-08-17
> **Related Notes:** [Indian_Polity_Constitution.md — Lecture 05](../GS_Polity_VR_Notes/Indian_Polity_Constitution.md)

### Active Recall Questions

<details><summary>What does Article 2 empower Parliament to do?</summary>

- Parliament by law can **admit** into the Union or **establish** new states as per conditions it deems fit.
- Article 2 deals with **external arrangement** — territory **outside** India.
</details>

<details><summary>What is the difference between "Admission" and "Establishment" under Art 2?</summary>

- **Admission:** Adding a **self-contained unit** (pre-existing territory with its own identity) to India. The territory had an identity before joining.
- **Establishment:** Creating/demarcating a **new territory** where none existed before, and adding it to India. India itself creates the identity.
- **Analogy:** A student taking *admission* at Vajiram (pre-existing identity) vs. Vajiram *establishing* a new branch (created from scratch).
</details>

<details><summary>Has Article 2 ever been used in India's history? Why/Why not?</summary>

- **No.** Article 2 has **never been used**.
- **Reason:** Per Re Berubari Union (1960), to admit territory, you must first **acquire** it. Acquiring requires **special majority** (sovereign power under international law). Since special majority automatically subsumes simple majority (needed for admission), acquiring takes care of admission in one step.
- Therefore, Art 2 became **USELESS** (not meaningless — the provision exists but circumstances prevent its use).
- Sikkim and Goa were NOT admitted under Art 2 — they were **acquired** first.
</details>

<details><summary>Does Article 2 require the pre-conditions (President's recommendation & State opinion)?</summary>

- **No.** Art 2 does NOT require the pre-conditions that Art 3 requires.
- Pre-conditions (Recommendation of President + Opinion of State) apply only to Art 3 Bills.
</details>
