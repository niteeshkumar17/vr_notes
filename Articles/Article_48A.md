# Article 48A — Protection of Environment & Wildlife

<details>
<summary>Show Answer</summary>

* **Category**: Directive Principles of State Policy (Part IV)
* **Core Concept**: Directs the State to endeavor to protect and improve the environment and to safeguard the forests and wildlife of the country.
* **Elephant Corridors & Ecological Federalism**: <span style="color: #e53e3e;">SC (Aug 2026) directed a nationwide survey and ruled that no State can blockade traditional inter-state elephant corridors or deploy barbaric methods (fireballs, spikes), reinforcing State duty under Art 48A and Right of Passage for wildlife.</span>
