# Article 39(c) — DPSP: Prevention of Concentration of Wealth

> **Part:** IV — Directive Principles of State Policy
> **Subject:** No concentration of wealth and means of production

## Text
The State shall, in particular, direct its policy towards securing that the operation of the economic system does not result in the concentration of wealth and means of production to the common detriment.

## Key Points
- Falls under **Socialistic Provisions** of the Constitution.
- Aims at preventing **concentration of wealth** — ensuring that the economic system does not create extreme inequality.
- Reflects **Marxian element** within India's Democratic Socialism.
- Read with [Article 39(b)](Article_39b.md) for the complete socialistic directive.
- Particularly relevant in the context of **LPG reforms (1991)** — privatization creates concentration of wealth, which this Article seeks to prevent through state policy.
- **Not justiciable** (being a DPSP) but fundamental in governance.

## Relevant Context
- Marx's core concern: The bourgeois (owner class) grows at the cost of the proletariat (working class) → concentration of wealth = injustice.
- India's Democratic Socialism addresses this through welfare schemes, progressive taxation, and regulatory frameworks rather than abolishing private property.
