# Article 54: Election of President (Electoral College)

> **Tags:** `[GS-2: Executive - President of India, Part V Chapter 1]`
> **Last Updated:** 2026-08-17
> **Related Notes:** [Polity_Lec2_AbheyKumar_President.md](../GS_Polity_VR_Notes/Polity_Lec2_AbheyKumar_President.md)

### Active Recall Questions

<details><summary>Who are the members of the Electoral College for the Presidential Election under Article 54?</summary>

- **Elected members of both Houses of Parliament** (Lok Sabha and Rajya Sabha).
- **Elected members of the Legislative Assemblies (MLAs)** of the States.
- **Elected members of Legislative Assemblies of Union Territories of Delhi and Puducherry** (added by the **70th Constitutional Amendment Act, 1992**).
</details>

<details><summary>Who is EXCLUDED from the Presidential Electoral College?</summary>

- **Nominated members** of Lok Sabha and Rajya Sabha.
- **Nominated members** of State Legislative Assemblies.
- **Members of State Legislative Councils (MLCs)** (both elected and nominated) — because they are not directly elected by the entire state electorate.
- **Nominated members of Delhi and Puducherry Assemblies**.
- <span style="color: #e53e3e;">**Jammu & Kashmir UT Legislative Assembly:** As of 2026, though the J&K Reorganisation Act 2019 created a Legislative Assembly for UT of J&K, Article 54 has not yet been amended to include J&K in the constitutional Electoral College.</span>
</details>

<details><summary>Why are nominated members and MLCs excluded from electing the President?</summary>

- The President is the Head of the Indian Republic and represents the entire sovereign people of India.
- Nominated members are executive appointees, and MLCs are elected by restricted sectoral electorates (teachers, graduates, local bodies) rather than universal adult franchise.
</details>
