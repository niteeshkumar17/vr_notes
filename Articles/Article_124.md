# Article 124 — Establishment and Constitution of Supreme Court

> **Part V — The Union, Chapter IV — The Union Judiciary**

## Text (Key Clauses)

**124. Establishment and constitution of Supreme Court.**

(1) There shall be a Supreme Court of India consisting of a Chief Justice of India and, until Parliament by law prescribes a larger number, of not more than seven other Judges.

(2) Every Judge of the Supreme Court shall be appointed by the President by warrant under his hand and seal after consultation with such of the Judges of the Supreme Court and of the High Courts in the States as the President may deem necessary for the purpose and shall hold office until he attains the age of sixty-five years:

Provided that in the case of appointment of a Judge other than the Chief Justice, the Chief Justice of India shall always be consulted.

---

## Key Points

*   **Original sanctioned strength:** CJI + 7 = 8 judges (1950).
*   Parliament has power to **increase** the number of judges by law (Supreme Court (Number of Judges) Act, 1956).
*   **Current strength (2026):** CJI + 33 = 34 judges (proposed to increase to 38).
*   **Appointment:** By the President, after consultation with CJI and other SC/HC judges → evolved into the **Collegium System** (Three Judges Cases: 1981, 1993, 1998).
*   **NJAC (National Judicial Appointments Commission)** — established by 99th Amendment + NJAC Act 2014, struck down by SC in 2015 as unconstitutional (violated judicial independence).
*   **Retirement age:** 65 years for SC judges; 62 years for HC judges.
*   **Qualifications:** Must be a citizen of India + (a) Judge of HC for 5 years, or (b) Advocate of HC for 10 years, or (c) Distinguished jurist in the opinion of the President.
