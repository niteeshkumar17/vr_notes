# Article 71: Matters Relating to Election of President or Vice-President

> **Tags:** `[GS-2: Executive - President & Vice-President Election Disputes, Part V Chapter 1]`
> **Last Updated:** 2026-08-17
> **Related Notes:** [Polity_Lec2_AbheyKumar_President.md](../GS_Polity_VR_Notes/Polity_Lec2_AbheyKumar_President.md)

### Active Recall Questions

<details><summary>Which forum has the exclusive jurisdiction to inquire into and decide Presidential/VP election disputes?</summary>

- **Supreme Court of India ONLY** (Article 71(1)). High Courts have zero jurisdiction.
- The decision of the Supreme Court is **final**.
</details>

<details><summary>Can a Presidential election be challenged on the ground of vacancies in the Electoral College?</summary>

- **No.** Under Article 71(4), the election of a person as President or Vice-President **cannot be called in question on the ground of the existence of any vacancy** for whatever reason among the members of the Electoral College.
</details>

<details><summary>Who has the locus standi to file an election petition challenging a Presidential election?</summary>

- Under the *Presidential and Vice-Presidential Elections Act, 1952*, only:
  1. A **candidate** at such election, OR
  2. Any **twenty or more electors** (members of the Electoral College) joined together as petitioners.
- Ordinary citizens have **no locus standi**.
</details>

<details><summary>What happens to official acts done by the President if the Supreme Court declares the election void?</summary>

- <span style="color: #e53e3e;">**Article 71(2) Non-Invalidation Rule:** If the election of a person as President or Vice-President is declared void by the Supreme Court, acts done by him in the exercise and performance of the powers and duties of the office of President or Vice-President on or before the date of the decision of the Supreme Court **shall NOT be invalidated** by reason of that declaration (Doctrine of Prospective Invalidation / De Facto Doctrine).</span>
</details>
