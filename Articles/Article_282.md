# Article 282 — Discretionary Grants by the Union or States for Public Purposes

> **Part XII: Finance, Property, Contracts and Suits — Chapter I (Finance)**

<details>
<summary>Show Answer</summary>

## Constitutional Provision

* **Text:** The Union or a State may make any grants for any public purpose, notwithstanding that the purpose is not one with respect to which Parliament or the Legislature of the State, as the case may be, may make laws.
* <span style="color: #e53e3e;">**UPSC Prelims Mandatory Distinction:** Article 282 is a general spending power provision. Grants made under Article 282 are **DISCRETIONARY** and completely outside the statutory recommendations of the Finance Commission under Article 280.</span>
* <span style="color: #e53e3e;">**Key Trap Detail:** Unlike Article 275 (which is charged upon the Consolidated Fund of India and non-votable), Article 282 grants are **VOTED EXPENDITURE** subject to annual parliamentary/state assembly appropriation.</span>
* <span style="color: #e53e3e;">**Historical Usage:** Article 282 was heavily utilized by the Union government to transfer massive capital and revenue funds for Centrally Sponsored Schemes (CSS) planned by the erstwhile Planning Commission, overshadowing statutory transfers under Article 275.</span>

## Cross-References
* **Article 275:** Statutory Grants-in-aid (Charged on Consolidated Fund of India).
* **Article 280:** Finance Commission recommendations.
