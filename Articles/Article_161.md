# Article 161 — Power of Governor to Grant Pardons, etc.

> **Part VI — The States, Chapter II — The Executive**

<details>
<summary>Show Answer</summary>

## Text

**161. Power of Governor to grant pardons, etc., and to suspend, remit or commute sentences in certain cases.**

The Governor of a State shall have the power to grant pardons, reprieves, respites or remissions of punishment or to suspend, remit or commute the sentence of any person convicted of any offence against any law relating to a matter to which the executive power of the State extends.

---

## Key Points

*   **Parallel to Article 72** — but narrower in scope.
*   **Scope:** Only offences under **State law** (matters within State executive power).
*   **Cannot pardon death sentence:** Unlike the President, the Governor CANNOT pardon a death sentence. Only the **President** can pardon death sentences (Art 72(1)(c)).
*   **Can commute death sentence:** Governor CAN commute death → life imprisonment (changes nature, not removes conviction).
*   **No Court Martial jurisdiction:** Unlike the President (who is Supreme Commander of Armed Forces), Governor has no power over Court Martial sentences.
*   **Court hierarchy irrelevant:** Whether the sentence was passed by High Court, Supreme Court, or any court — if the offence is under State law, Governor has jurisdiction. Court hierarchy does not matter.

---

## President (Art 72) vs Governor (Art 161) Comparison

| Feature | President (Art 72) | Governor (Art 161) |
|---------|-------------------|-------------------|
| Law scope | Union law | State law |
| Court Martial | ✅ Yes | ❌ No |
| Death sentence — Pardon | ✅ Yes | ❌ No |
| Death sentence — Commute | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes |
| Applicable courts | All courts | All courts |
| Discretion word | NOT in constitution | Explicitly in Art 163 |

## UPSC Relevance

*   Prelims: "Governor cannot pardon death sentence" — TRUE. "Governor has no mercy powers" — FALSE (common trap).
*   Mains: Compare mercy powers of President vs Governor; discuss whether Governor's clemency power is subject to judicial review.
*   <span style="color: #e53e3e;">**UPSC Trap:** "Governor cannot pardon" does NOT mean Governor has zero mercy powers. Governor can commute, remit, respite, reprieve — Governor specifically cannot **pardon death sentence** but can **commute** it (death → life).</span>
