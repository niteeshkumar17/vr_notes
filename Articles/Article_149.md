# Article 149 — Duties and Powers of the Comptroller and Auditor-General

<details>
<summary>Show Answer</summary>

## Text & Constitutional Provisions (Simplified)

* The CAG shall perform such duties and exercise such powers in relation to the **accounts of the Union and of the States** and of any other authority or body as may be prescribed by or under any law made by Parliament.
* **Governing Legislation:** Enacted under this article, the **Comptroller and Auditor-General's (Duties, Powers and Conditions of Service) Act, 1971 (DPC Act)** governs the audit mandate.

## Key Points for UPSC
* **Three Types of Audits Conducted:**
  1. **Financial Audit / Regularity Audit:** Verifying that accounts are properly kept and financial statements present a true and fair view.
  2. **Compliance Audit / Legality Audit:** Ensuring that expenditure conforms to the laws, rules, and budgetary sanctions enacted by the legislature.
  3. **Performance / Value for Money (VFM) Audit:** Assessing the Economy, Efficiency, and Effectiveness (3 Es) of government programmes and schemes (e.g. Green India Mission, MGNREGS, Defense procurements).
* **Audit of Bodies/Authorities:** Substantially financed bodies, government companies under the Companies Act, and statutory corporations whose acts provide for CAG audit.
* **Separation of Accounts from Audit:** In 1976, accounting was separated from auditing at the Union level (CGA under Ministry of Finance prepares accounts; CAG only audits). At State level, CAG still handles both compilation of accounts and audit in most States.
