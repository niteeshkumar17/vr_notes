# Article 312 of the Indian Constitution

**Part:** Part XIV (Services Under the Union and the States)

**Heading:** All-India Services

**Key Provisions:**
*   Provides for the creation of one or more All India Services (including an all-India judicial service) common to the Union and the States.
*   The Rajya Sabha must pass a resolution supported by not less than two-thirds of the members present and voting declaring that it is necessary or expedient in the national interest to do so.
*   The Indian Administrative Service (IAS) and the Indian Police Service (IPS) are deemed to be services created by Parliament under this article.

**Relevance:** Highly relevant in matters concerning the Indian Police Service (IPS) and their deputation or leadership roles across various Central Armed Police Forces (CAPFs) and state police departments.
