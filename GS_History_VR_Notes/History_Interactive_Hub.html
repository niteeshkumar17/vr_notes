Political Transformation & Cause-Effect Arrows (1707–1761)

1. Death of Aurangzeb (1707)

Mughal power vacuum ➔ Succession war between 3 sons ➔ Muazzam wins as Bahadur Shah I ➔ Shahuji released in 1707 to induce Maratha civil conflict.

⬇️

2. Rise of Zulfiqar Khan & Noble Domination (1712)

Zulfiqar Khan crushes 3 rival princes under elephants ➔ Jahandar Shah made Emperor ➔ Zulfiqar becomes PM ➔ Abolishes Jaziya in Gujarat for local backing.

⬇️

3. Sayyid Brothers Kingmakers & Maratha Coalition (1713–1719)

Sayyid Brothers execute Zulfiqar Khan ➔ Golden Farman issued to EIC (1717) ➔ Balaji Vishwanath proposes Dual Governance ➔ Sayyid Brothers & Marathas execute Farrukh Siyar (1719).

⬇️

4. Nadir Shah Invasion & Regional Collapse (1739–1761)

Muhammad Shah Rangeela neglects governance ➔ Nadir Shah invasion & Battle of Karnal (1739), loots Peacock Throne ➔ 8 Successor states break away ➔ 3rd Battle of Panipat (1761).
