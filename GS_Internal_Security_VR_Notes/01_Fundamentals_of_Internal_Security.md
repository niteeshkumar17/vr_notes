## 2. 1947–48: First internal-security opening — J&K (IS-01-02)

**Two-Nation Theory** → **India** and **Pakistan**. **Jammu and Kashmir (J&K)** chose to stay **independent** (not accede to either). Muslim-majority state, Hindu ruler **Maharaja Hari Singh**.

**Trigger (class):** revolt always needs a spark. Here: **Pathans and Scouts** in the **north-western** erstwhile state were unhappy with the Maharaja’s **revenue / taxes**. Pakistan supported the revolt. That **forced** Hari Singh to sign the **Instrument of Accession** — **surrendered sovereignty to India**.

**Supreme Court (class: 2024):** after abrogation of **Article 370** and the **J&K reorganisation (2019)**, once Accession was signed, sovereignty stood surrendered; **J&K is an integral part of India**. Special constitutional arrangement followed — **polity class later** (class said not to dive today).

**Nehru took a bilateral fight to the United Nations (UN)** → **UN Resolution 47** (Kashmir Resolution) → a **ceasefire line (CFL)**.

By the time Accession could be completed, India had already **lost about 1/3** of J&K to Pakistan (**2/3 with India**).

**CFL:** from the **Jammu sector** up to the **southern tip** of the triangular **Siachen** glacier. Not demarcated beyond that: conditions **uninhabitable**; even today deployment at **about −45 °C** is a Herculean task.

## 3. CFL → Line of Control; Siachen (IS-01-03)

After the **1971** war (**Bangladesh Liberation**; class: third war with Pakistan after **1947–48** and **1965**), the **Shimla Agreement** **rechristened** the same CFL as the **Line of Control (LoC)**. **No cartographic difference** — only the name, before vs after Shimla.

**Operation Rajiv** and **Operation Meghdoot:** Indian Army took **NJ 9842**. Under Meghdoot, control of the stretch / rivers and tributaries from Siachen. That alignment = **Actual Ground Position Line (AGPL)**, about **110 km**, still contested.

**Siachen:** highest battlefield; about **2,600 km²**; a **choke point** — whoever holds it has oversight toward **Srinagar / Leh** (class). Under Indian occupancy.

## 5. Insurgency; Naga (1952) (IS-01-05)

> **Insurgency** is an act of **armed rebellion** by a **group of citizens**, usually with a **political ambition** (**sovereignty or autonomy**), **with the support of a hostile element**. Example for the North-East: **China and Pakistan**.

**1950s** — next decade after the first border opening. Roots of North-East insurgency = **Naga insurgency (1952)** (first general election year). Leader **A.Z. Phizo**: declared **independence from India**; **Naga Federal Republic** + **Naga Federal Army**.

**Demand:** separate sovereign region **Nagalim** = hilly tracts of **Manipur** + parts of **Myanmar** + **Naga Hills district**.

**North-East political origin (class):** **United Assam** + princely **Tripura, Manipur, Sikkim** + **North-East Frontier Agency (NEFA)** (today’s **Arunachal Pradesh**), administered by Assam. **Majority of NE states were carved from United Assam.** Naga Hills was in United Assam.

**1963:** **Nagaland** created on the **16-point Agreement (1960)**. **Article 371** special privileges (Nagaland’s provision is **371A**). Still did not satisfy the Naga groups.

**2021 — National Socialist Council of Nagaland (Isak-Muivah) (NSCN-IM)** to the Union Home Minister: **separate Constitution** + **separate flag** (both = **sovereignty**). **Still in 2026, Nagalim demands exist.**

## 6. Mizo, Assam / United Liberation Front of Asom (ULFA), Bodo (IS-01-06)

**Mizo (Lushai Hills / Mizoram):** flowering of **bamboo** → explosion of **rats** → food stock hit → **1959 famine (Mautam)** → **Mizo Famine Front** under **Laldenga** → **Mizoram** **1972 Union Territory (UT)** → **1987 statehood**.

**Assam:** **illegal migration** → demography / **demographic re-engineering** → internal conflict (jobs + cultural transgression). **ULFA** — demand **Swadhin Assam** (sovereign Assam); sheet: **southern Assam**. Detail later.

**Bodo:** demand **Bodoland**. Detail later.

Insurgency from **1952** onward, then an unprecedented rise of related developments.

## 7. Naxalism (1960s) and why “Naxal-free” is early (IS-01-07)

**Naxalbari, West Bengal (WB), 1967:** **violent peasant uprising against zamindars** (majority of the peasants were tribal). Inspired by **Mao**. Spread quickly to **Uttar Pradesh (UP), Bihar (BR), WB, Madhya Pradesh (MP), Odisha (OR / Odisha)**. **Chhattisgarh** as a separate state did not yet exist.

**Class trap:** Naxalbari is only an **identifier**. Naxalism is **not a one-day event**. It is the **culmination of tribal uprisings** from **pre-independence** (class listed Kol, Bhil, Santhal, etc.; **~110** tribal uprisings) that **continued after 1947** (Telangana struggle **1946–51**, Koel-Karo in Bihar, Bhoomi Sena in Maharashtra, Naxalbari 1967). **Naxalbari = episodic** in that chain.

**Still live (class):** Niyamgiri (Odisha, 2024); Ken–Betwa “fast until death” (recent); **March 2026** Palghar and Nashik (Maharashtra) tribals denied forest **title / ownership**. Until tribal discontent is resolved, the issue continues.

**Home Minister / “Mission Naxal Mukt Bharat”:** class heard India called **Naxal-free this year** on the floor. Class: **too early**. Operations alone cannot finish it — **dialogue + development** must run **parallel** to the offensive.

**Bihar — Bhojpur:** “red hot spot.” **Dala system** — dominant **Kshatriyas / Bhumihars** holding majority land. Sheet: first-night custom with the zamindar. Peasants **joined the Naxalites**. **Ranvir Sena** (same caste bloc) — **Supreme Court (SC) banned** it.

**Chhattisgarh — Salwa Judum:** “peace-keeping force” developed by the **Chhattisgarh government**. **SC banned** it. Divided villages into **support Naxals** vs **against Naxals**.

**Unemployment (Mains hook):** high unemployment is dangerous for IS. Unemployed youth: **rejection of societal norms** → easier **exit into crime** + **radicalisation** by anti-social elements. In Naxal areas, **majority of the peripheral cadre** are **unemployed** (core cadre vs peripheral cadre).

## 8. Khalistan (1970s–80s) and Kashmir 1987 (IS-01-08)

**Khalistan** = **land of the pure**. **Jarnail Singh Bhindranwale.** Demand: **separate Sikh nation**. Built first on a **Hindu–Sikh divide**. Detail of causes = later class.

**Counter (class):** **Operation Blue Star**, **Armed Forces (Special Powers) Act (AFSPA)**, **Gill doctrine**. Class takes **1997** as the close — **AFSPA withdrawn** from Punjab; no longer a disturbed area — **not** 1984–85 as the end-year.

**1987 Kashmir insurgency:** **rigging of the Assembly election** → **Jammu Kashmir Liberation Front (JKLF)** separatist movement.

**Five traditional IS threats (sheet listed “4” then five):**

## 9. Non-traditional threats; social media (IS-01-09)

**Organised crime** has **no universal definition** because of its **evolving** nature.

| Item | Class / sheet |
|:---|:---|
| **Drug / human / arms trafficking** | All organised crime. |
| **Money laundering** | Concealing the identity of **illegally obtained** money by passing it through **complex financial channels** so the output **appears legitimate**. Not the lay line “black into white.” **Black money** = no receipt. |
| **Hawala** | **Trust-based** system; **transfer money without physical movement**. Crypto = a form of **digital hawala**. |
| **Dead-drop email** | Mail is **written but not sent** (stays in drafts). Login is shared; the other party reads it. Agencies intercept **communication that has taken place** — a dead drop never “sends.” Class linked it to a **Delhi blast** example. |
| **Ransomware** | Intruder **breaches** a system, **takes control of data**, demands **ransom** (often **crypto**) to return control. **WannaCry** named as a famous ransomware. |
| **Digital arrest** | Impersonation of **law enforcement** to create urgency by intimidation. |
| **Pig butchering** | Lures via **investment fraud, Ponzi, job scams**. |
| **Honey trap** | Faking online romance. |
| **Pretexting** | Trying to extract sensitive information by a false claim. |

**Social media and security**

**Mob lynching** ← **fake news** → misinfo → confusion → **political agenda** / **illegitimate business gain**. Also **xenophobia**. **Social engineering** → manipulation of mindset → **radicalisation / religious indoctrination**. Platforms are **decentralised**; **cadre strength** (boxed on the sheet).

**Orwellian world** — class: *Nineteen Eighty-Four* was **fiction** (1980s publication in class talk); now used as a lens on surveillance / information control. (Margin scribble on the sheet is unclear — not invented here.)

Closing Q from class: 1947–48 Pathan/Scout revolt was **revenue/tax**, **not** framed as a religious war. A sharper **Hindu–Muslim** split in Kashmir is more a **1990s** story.
